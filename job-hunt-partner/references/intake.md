# Intake

The intake turns a stranger into a profile you can work from. It should feel like a quick conversation with a good recruiter, not a form.

## How to run it

1. Open with one line that says why you are asking: "A few quick questions so the advice fits your field and situation. Skip anything you'd rather not share."
2. Ask Batch A. Then ask only the later batches the current task needs (see the table in SKILL.md).
3. Up to four questions per turn. Offer options where the answers are predictable, and always allow a free answer.
4. If the user attached a resume or gave a LinkedIn or portfolio link, read it first and pre-fill what you can. Then ask them to confirm instead of asking from scratch. "Looks like you're a mid-level data analyst in Lisbon, targeting analytics roles. Right?" saves three questions.
5. Never ask for anything you do not need: no ID numbers, no date of birth, no salary history.
6. When done, show the profile block below, filled in, and offer to save it.

## Batch A: always

1. **Field and target roles.** "What do you do, and which job titles are you going after?" Ask for two to five titles, since the same job goes by several names.
2. **Level.** Years of experience and current or last title. Ask whether they want the same level, a step up, or are open to a step down (career changers often are).
3. **Location and work rights.** Where they are based, and where they can legally work without sponsorship. Ask whether visa sponsorship is needed anywhere.
4. **Goal for today.** What they want out of this session.

## Batch B: finding and scoring roles

1. **Work setup.** Remote, hybrid, on-site, and which regions or time zones work.
2. **Relocation.** No, yes with sponsorship, or yes.
3. **Pay floor.** Minimum acceptable pay, with currency and period. "Skip" is a valid answer.
4. **Industries.** Ones to favour and ones to avoid. Add company size or stage preferences if they have them.
5. **Dealbreakers.** Anything that rules a role out: travel, contract type, shift work, specific employers.

## Batch C: materials

1. **Resume or CV.** Ask them to attach the most complete version they have (the master copy, not a version cut for one job).
2. **Proof of work.** Portfolio, LinkedIn, GitHub, personal site, publications, writing samples, whatever their field uses.
3. **Missing work.** "Is there real work that isn't on your resume, such as freelance, contract, side projects or volunteering?"
4. **What to lead with.** "What are you strongest at, in your own words?" Use their words. Do not invent a personal brand.

## Batch D: outreach

1. **LinkedIn plan.** Free or Premium. This changes the note limits (see outreach.md).
2. **Network assets.** Schools, past employers, communities, bootcamps or meetups whose people might help.
3. **Language.** Which languages to write outreach in.
4. **Existing threads.** Anyone at their target companies they already talk to on any channel.

## Batch E: tracking

1. **Tool.** Notion, Google Sheets, Airtable, Excel, something else, or nothing yet.
2. **Link or file.** For a connected app, the database or sheet link. If they have nothing, offer to set up a simple tracker (see tracking.md).
3. **What to log.** Default is only roles worth pursuing. Ask if they also want rejected roles logged.

## Batch F: style (optional, ask once)

1. How long they want answers: short, medium or detailed.
2. Tone for messages: formal, neutral or casual.
3. Any rules of their own, such as words to avoid, or a confidence rating at the end of each answer.

## Sensitive situations

Some users have a visa constraint, a nationality that triggers sanctions screening at some employers, a career gap, a health matter or a career change. Do not probe. Ask once, gently: "Anything that affects where you can work, or how you want to present your history? Share only what you want me to use." Use what they share only where it changes the advice, and never put it in outreach unless they ask.

## The profile block

Show it filled in. Leave unknown fields as "not given" rather than guessing.

```
JOB SEARCH PROFILE (updated YYYY-MM-DD)
Name:
Field:
Target titles:
Level: years, current or last title, level wanted
Based in:
Can work in (no sponsorship):
Sponsorship needed for:
Work setup: remote / hybrid / on-site, regions or time zones
Relocation:
Pay floor:
Industries, favour:
Industries, avoid:
Dealbreakers:
Materials: resume, portfolio, LinkedIn, other links
Work missing from resume:
Lead strengths (their words):
LinkedIn plan: free / Premium
Network assets:
Outreach languages:
Tracker: tool and link
Log rejected roles: yes / no
Response style:
Notes the user chose to share:
```

After showing it, ask: "Want me to save this so next time we skip the questions?" Save only on a yes. If the environment has no memory, tell them to paste the block at the start of their next chat, or keep it in a project's instructions.
